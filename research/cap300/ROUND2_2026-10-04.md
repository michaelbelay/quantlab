# CAP300 Round 2 — redesigns after Round 1 (pre-registered 2026-10-04, before any Round 2 run)

## What Round 1 showed (round1_results.json)
All 10 designs lost on the 280 unseen coins, **and so did their random twins** (median twin ends at ~25 of 300 USDT):
full exposure to a handful of smaller altcoins loses ~90% over 2021-26 whatever is selected. The trend *selection* still
beat random (LT-K30-N8-weekly p98, LT-K15-N8 p95). Diagnosis: the edge exists, beta to falling altcoins swamps it.

## Redesign A — single-asset time-series trend (natural fit for 300 USDT: 1-3 positions)
Rule (fixed): signal = sign of the 20-day return (long-only variant: max(sign, 0)); position sized to 40% annual
volatility (30-day realised), capped at 2x; decided on the close, held the next day; costs 0.10% per side on every
change in position (2x reported); funding charged. Variants: `SA-L` long-only daily, `SA-LS` long/short daily,
`SA-L-W` / `SA-LS-W` the same rebalanced weekly.
**Test of generality:** the rule run on **each of the 280 unseen coins separately** (independent markets). Reported:
share of coins with positive return, pooled trade PF, median annualised Sharpe, and a **sign-randomisation baseline per
coin** (same exposure schedule, random direction each holding period; 200 replicas) — share of coins beating p95.
**Deployable form:** the same rule on BTC, ETH, SOL (most liquid, 50/20/5 USDT minimums), one third of 300 USDT each,
simulated with `sim.py`. BTC/ETH/SOL are development coins, so this part is reference, not validation.

## Redesign B — risk-adjusted momentum, low exposure
Among the 30 most liquid eligible coins: score = 60-day return / 60-day volatility; hold the top 5 with score > 0,
equal weight, **total exposure 50%** (30 USDT each at 300), exit early on a close below the prior 20-day low,
BTC > 50-day SMA gate. Same unseen-coin account test and 200 random twins as Round 1.

## Classes
A (generality): 🟢 if ≥ 60% of unseen coins positive AND pooled PF ≥ 1.10 AND ≥ 25% of coins beat their p95 sign-random
baseline (vs 5% expected by chance) AND pooled PF at 2x costs ≥ 1.05; 🟡 if ≥ 55% positive and pooled PF ≥ 1.05;
🟠 if > 50% positive or pooled PF > 1.0; 🔴 otherwise.
B: Round 1 classes unchanged.
