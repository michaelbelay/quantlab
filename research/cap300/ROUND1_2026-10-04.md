# CAP300 Round 1 — strategies built for a 300 USDT futures account (pre-registered 2026-10-04)

Standing rule (Michael, 2026-10-04): find the best strategy that works on the **existing 300 USDT**. Discovery is
creative; validation is strict.

## Account model (`sim.py`)
300 USDT start, leverage 1x (1.5x cap only for the vol-target design), daily decisions on the close, fee 0.05% +
slippage 0.05% per side (2x reported), real funding, per-coin minimum notional (BTC 50, ETH 20, measured mainnet;
others 5 USDT): orders below the minimum are not placed, adjustments below it are skipped. Drawdown and returns
are on the 300 USDT account.

## Designs (10, all long-only with the BTC > 50-day SMA gate; selection by liquidity, never by recent return)
| Id | Idea |
|---|---|
| LT-K15-N5 / N8, LT-K30-N5 / N8 | trend (20-day return > 0) among the 15/30 most liquid coins; hold up to 5/8, most liquid first; inverse-vol weights, 100% invested |
| LT-K30-N8-weekly | same, rebalanced weekly (fewer orders) |
| LT-K30-N8-VT40 | same, scaled to 40% annual volatility, ≤ 1.5x |
| MAJ5-trend | the 5 most liquid coins only |
| DON55-K30-N5 / DON20-K30-N5 | Donchian breakout 55/20 and 20/10 days, up to 5 equal positions |
| PB-K30-N5 | buy pullbacks below the 5-day average inside an uptrend, up to 5 equal positions |

## Test
- **Decisive:** the 280 unseen coins (`research/pipeline/oos_universe.json`), BTC as the gate only.
- Reference: the live account's 127-pair universe (development data — informative, never validating).
- **Random twin** (selection skill): same daily position count, same exposure, same BTC-gate days, coins drawn at
  random from the same liquid pool; 200 replicas; percentile of final equity.
- DSR recorded as a diagnostic.

## Classes (unseen coins)
- 🟢 **Validated:** PF ≥ 1.10, PF at 2x costs ≥ 1.05, max DD ≤ 40%, ≥ 3/4 regimes positive, random twin ≥ p95, ≥ 5/8 half-year folds positive.
- 🟡 **Research lead:** PF ≥ 1.10 and random twin ≥ p90 and max DD ≤ 50%, failing something else.
- 🟠 **Weak signal:** PF > 1.0 or random twin ≥ p75.
- 🔴 **Rejected:** otherwise.
Every 🟡/🟠 gets a redesign in Round 2.
