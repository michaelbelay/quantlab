## The frozen settings (copied, not re-chosen)
From s7_pit_fit.py's printed choice on FIT 2021-2023: **dip 1.5 sigma, hold 72 h, stop 3 sigma, trend 10 days.**

On 4h bars (1h windows divided by 4, so the calendar length is identical):
- **daily move** `sig` = sd of 4h log returns over the last 180 bars (720 h) x sqrt(6);
- **uptrend**: 10-day return > 0, i.e. close > close 60 bars ago;
- **dip**: 24h return <= **-1.5 x sig**, i.e. close / close 6 bars ago - 1 <= -1.5 sig;
- **long** at that bar's close; **stop** at entry x (1 - 3 sig); **exit** at the 18-bar (72 h) hold;
- one trade per coin at a time, 18-bar cooldown after a trade closes.
