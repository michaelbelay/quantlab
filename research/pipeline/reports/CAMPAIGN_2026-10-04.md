## 10. Caveats

- Every result is a backtest. Promising/validated here means *worth a forward test*, not *approved for live trading*.
- Development coins were mined by earlier research too (≈ 8,000 configurations); only the out-of-sample stage is clean.
- Each strategy is one random draw of parameters from its family; a family that fails here may still contain a working variant.
- Random-entry replicas ignore funding; this makes the baseline slightly easier to beat, not harder.
- Portfolio drawdown uses a simple compounding 10-slot model (no correlation cap), comparable across strategies, not a live forecast.
## 11. DSR diagnosis (computed 2026-10-04 after the campaign)

Daily Sharpe across the 238 campaign strategies: mean −0.054, sd 0.048; 89% are negative. With 8,264 trials counted, the deflated-Sharpe hurdle (expected best Sharpe from that many tries of noise) is **3.52 annualised**. The best strategy reached 1.07. DSR = 0 for everything therefore says *"nothing here beats the luck of 8,000+ tries"*, which is true but cannot rank candidates. If the variance were taken only from the 27 positive-Sharpe trials, the hurdle would be 1.06 annualised, and the top two (1.07, 1.06) would sit right on it. Recorded, not blocking, as decided.

## 12. Interpretation (Claude, not computed; read with the tables above)

1. **The edge gate is where nearly everything dies** (233/236). It is not the luck correction (non-blocking) and not costs: 98% have profit factor < 1.15 *before* doubling costs. Most mechanisms tested simply lose money on 1h crypto with realistic costs.
2. **Beating random entries is not the same as making money.** 61 strategies beat 95% of random-entry copies, but most of them are shorts or filters that lose *less* than random entries in the same direction. Only 4 cleared PF ≥ 1.15.
3. **Shorts: 0 of 59 profitable**, and inside two-sided strategies the short legs lost more than the long legs (−379k vs −291k nominal; short leg profitable in 4/53). This confirms the earlier 0/28 short-family result on a fresh, larger sample.
4. **Long-side leads share one weakness:** they make money in 2021 and 2023-24 and lose in 2022 and/or 2025-26 (regimes 2/4). This is the profile of *being long a rising market*, the same trap the cloud sweep found.
5. **The single most interesting result: `trend_continuation · vol_expanding · both`** (trend-only regime gate, structure stop, exit on the opposite signal). PF 1.20 (1.135 at 2x costs), t 3.76, beat **all** random replicas, **positive in all 4 regimes** (bear 2022 included), 79/133 coins, parameter neighbours robust. It failed **only** the portfolio-drawdown gate (48.8% vs 40%). It is a single random draw; its neighbourhood (stops, holding time, filters, risk per slot) has not been explored.
6. **New families:** `funding_extreme` was the most *consistent* family (5/10 PF > 1, 8/10 beat random), but with thin edges and deep drawdowns. `rel_strength` produced 2 of the 3 G2 survivors, both failing on regimes (lose in 2022). `combo`, `structure_break` and `regime_switch` showed nothing beyond chance.
7. **Drawdowns are the second wall:** among the 26 profitable strategies, median 10-slot drawdown is 82%; one (a 3-trade fluke) is within 40%.
8. **Nothing reached the out-of-sample coins**, so this campaign produced **no validated and no promising strategy**. Nothing is a candidate for live trading.

**Suggested next steps (not run):** (a) a focused neighbourhood search around `trend_continuation · vol_expanding · both`, aimed at drawdown (fewer slots, a correlation cap, smaller risk per trade); (b) a focused search on `funding_extreme` long variants; (c) lower the short share further.
