# TREND_GATED, concentrated: top 20 × 15 USDT (pre-registered 2026-10-04)

Requested by Michael after TREND_GATED's validation run: the original holds ~61 coins at ~1 USDT each on a
300 USDT account, below Binance's 5 USDT minimum. This variant fits the live account's existing 20 × 15 USDT setup.

## Rules (fixed now)
- Universe and eligibility as TREND_GATED (`strats.eligible`: listed ≥ 60 days, 30-day mean daily volume ≥ 5M USDT).
- Daily, decided on the close of day t, held over day t+1 (cloud engine timing, costs and funding).
- Trend score = 20-day return. Candidates: eligible coins with score > 0.
- Hold the **top 20 by score**, **equal weight 5% of equity each** (15 USDT at 300 USDT). Fewer than 20 candidates → the rest stays in cash.
- Only while **BTC close > its 50-day average** (same gate); otherwise flat.
- Rebalanced daily. Costs 0.10% per side (fee + slippage); 2x costs reported.

## Test
- **Decisive:** the 280 unseen coins (`oos_universe.json`); BTC used only as the gate.
- Reported for reference: the live account's 127-pair universe (development data, not a validation).

## Pass bar (unseen coins, all required)
PF ≥ 1.10 · PF at 2x costs ≥ 1.05 · max drawdown ≤ 40% · ≥ 3 of 4 regimes positive (2021, 2022, 2023-24, 2025-26).

## Decision rule
Pass → candidate for a old-bot version (parity-checked), then Michael decides on live. Fail → recorded with the numbers.
